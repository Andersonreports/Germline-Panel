Metagenomics

Metagenomics involves the direct analysis of genetic material from environmental or clinical samples, allowing identification of microbial communities without the need for culturing. Our metagenomics panels cover 16S metataxonomic profiling, gut microbial analysis, and whole genome metagenomic sequencing.
